# Whitepaper Record (Canonical Layer 1)

Faithful record of the **published** MoonPod Whitepaper **v1.1 (August 2025)**,
`https://moonpod.space/whitepaper.html`. This is the canonical reference. Items here are part
of the official record unless Rowland formally replaces them via a versioned update.

## Positioning
A decentralized space-exploration project letting global participants collectively fund and
govern lunar missions. Genesis Pods are NFT-backed claims intended to secure payload slots
aboard real Moon-bound deliveries.

## Phases
1. Own a Genesis Pod.
2. Launch $MOONPOD with staking and governance.
3. Deploy payloads with aerospace partners.
4. Pursue moon-rock retrieval, sample distribution, real-world returns.

## Genesis Pods
- Total **888**: public **750 @ 0.1 ETH**, premium **88 @ 0.25 ETH+**, reserved **50** (team & partners).
- Each Pod described as securing physical lunar payload space; permitted content etched into
  lightweight material and included in a lunar delivery. ERC-721 with evolving metadata.

## Genesis proceeds allocation
60% aerospace/payload · 25% technology & smart contracts · 10% community ops & marketing ·
5% legal & compliance. **Do not substitute a different allocation without a formal update.**

## Token strategy (what the whitepaper does and does NOT say)
The whitepaper **does not publish**: final supply, contract address, presale price, final
allocation table, hard cap, soft cap, vesting schedule, staking design, or liquidity plan.
It states Phase One has no token; Phase Two introduces $MOONPOD with governance and staking;
and the token should launch aligned with mission milestones, not rushed for liquidity.

> Any numeric tokenomics outside the whitepaper must be labeled a founder-approved spec, a prior
> planning decision, or an unresolved proposal — never presented as being *in* the whitepaper.

## Technology (target capabilities, not proof of deployment)
Ethereum mainnet contracts; ERC-721 Pods with evolving metadata; OpenZeppelin governance;
possible future Polygon/Arbitrum; cryptographic proof of payload inclusion; mission tracking;
certified aerospace logistics providers.
