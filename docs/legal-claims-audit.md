# Legal & Claims Audit

A disclaimer that a token is "not a security" does **not** determine its legal status. MoonPod
intends to sell digital assets and use funds for future development and physical mission
activity. This document compares each public claim against the evidence supporting it. **Legal
review is required before enabling any public sale.**

## Prohibited marketing (must never appear)
Guarantees of: profit, price appreciation, exchange listing, yield, airdrops, launch, lunar
delivery, sample return, liquidity, or resale value. The corrected site removes these and adds
explicit risk language.

## Required legal review scope (before public sale)
Token sale structure; marketing language; purchaser eligibility; jurisdiction restrictions;
KYC/AML; refund language; mission-risk disclosure; payload-service terms; IP; consumer
protection; privacy; tax treatment; aerospace/export-control where applicable.
