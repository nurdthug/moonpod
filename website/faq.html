Frequently Asked Questions

Updated: July 17, 2025

What is MoonPod?

MoonPod is a crypto-native space project allowing community members to purchase Genesis Pods for a real lunar payload mission. Each pod represents keepsake space aboard a lunar-bound mission, combining physical space exploration with Web3 community governance.

How many Genesis Pods are available?

888 Genesis Pods total: 750 public sale (0.1 ETH each), 88 premium tier (0.25+ ETH), and 50 reserved for team/partnerships. This limited supply ensures exclusivity and community value.

What's the pricing structure?

Standard Genesis Pods: 0.1 ETH each. Premium pods offer enhanced payload space and additional governance weight. All transactions are processed on-chain to our treasury address.

Is this a real lunar mission?

Yes. MoonPod partners with established aerospace companies to secure verified payload space on upcoming lunar missions. Pod holders receive mission updates and proof of payload deployment.

Will there be a MoonPod token?

Not immediately. Our "no token on day one" approach prioritizes mission success over speculation. Future tokenization will align with mission milestones and be exclusively available to Genesis Pod holders first.

What can I include in my payload?

Digital Content: Personal messages, family photos, artwork, music files, code snippets, poems, children's drawings (digitized), social media posts, memes, or crypto wallet signatures.

Physical Items (micro-scale): Hair strands, fingernail clippings, pressed flowers, seeds, sand from meaningful locations, wedding ring engravings (laser-etched), handwritten notes on space-grade paper, or family heirloom fragments.

Personal Items: Coordinates of birthplaces, timestamps of special moments, DNA data, fingerprints, voice recordings, or cultural symbols representing heritage.

Creative Submissions: Original art, short stories, business logos, academic research, scientific formulas, or time capsule letters to future generations.

All items must meet strict aerospace safety standards and weight limits (typically under 1 gram). Detailed submission guidelines provided to Genesis Pod holders.

Who's behind MoonPod?

MoonPod was conceived and developed by Rowland Akinduro, the visionary creator behind the project. As CEO of Opin Global and lead developer, Rowland combines deep blockchain expertise with space exploration passion to bring this lunar payload concept to life.

Co-founder Brian Mock provides financial strategy and operational expertise, ensuring the project's sustainability and professional execution. Together, they balance innovative vision with disciplined implementation.

How do I verify my pod purchase?

All purchases are recorded on-chain at our treasury address. You can verify transactions on Etherscan. Pod holders receive additional confirmation via email and Discord.

What blockchain networks are supported?

Currently Ethereum Mainnet for all purchases. We support MetaMask and major Ethereum wallets. Gas fees are separate from pod pricing.

When is the lunar mission launching?

Mission timeline depends on aerospace partners and regulatory approvals. Pod holders receive priority updates on launch schedules and mission progress through our community channels.

Are there governance benefits?

Yes. Genesis Pod holders get exclusive voting rights on mission parameters, future token mechanics, and community decisions. Premium pod holders receive enhanced governance weight.

Can I resell my Genesis Pod?

Once NFT contracts are deployed, pods become tradeable assets. Early adopters benefit from potential appreciation as mission milestones are achieved and community grows.

What about the return mission?

Phase 4 includes plans for lunar sample return missions. Genesis Pod holders get priority access to authenticated moon rock distributions and museum partnership benefits.

How do I stay updated?

Join our Discord, follow X (@MoonPodSpace), and Telegram. Pod holders receive exclusive updates via email at mission@moonpod.space with mission progress and community announcements.
