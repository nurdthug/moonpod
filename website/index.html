Mission Architecture

MoonPod operates through a four-phase deployment model, each phase building toward actual lunar payload delivery and ecosystem expansion.

Genesis Phase: Community formation through NFT ownership, establishing governance foundation and technical infrastructure.

Token Phase: $MOONPOD launch with staking mechanisms, enabling decentralized governance and community-driven mission planning.

Deployment Phase: Aerospace partnerships, payload manifest scheduling, and on-chain verification of launch milestones.

Return Phase: Lunar sample collection partnerships, artifact distribution, and educational collaboration with research institutions.
