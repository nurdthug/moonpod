How it works

1 · Genesis Pods

Genesis Pod NFTs are the original participation layer. The published whitepaper describes 888 Pods intended to secure lunar payload space. Mint availability and on-chain details are being verified — see the Genesis Pods section.

2 · The $MOONPOD token

A fixed-supply ERC-20 intended to provide access tiers, governance participation, and community utility. It will launch aligned with mission milestones, not rushed for liquidity. It is not deployed yet.

3 · Missions

The long-term goal is real lunar payload delivery through contracted aerospace and logistics providers. Any specific partner, manifest, or launch date will only be shown here once supported by documentary evidence.

FAQ

Is the $MOONPOD token live?

No. The token is in prelaunch and is not deployed to any network. There is no public contract address. When a contract exists, its verified address and block-explorer link will be shown here and read directly from the project's versioned deployment records.

Can I buy $MOONPOD now?

No. There is no live presale or purchase mechanism on this site. Please disregard any message — on any channel — claiming a sale is live until it is reflected here with a verified contract address.

What is the total supply?

The fixed total supply is 8,888,888,888 MOONPOD, approved on July 5, 2026. It will be verifiable on-chain once the contract is deployed and its source is verified.

What chain will it use?

Ethereum is the intended settlement layer. The exact network and any future cross-chain plans will be confirmed at launch.

How do Genesis Pods relate to the token?

Genesis Pods are the original NFT participation layer. Holders are intended to receive benefits within the ecosystem. Specific benefits will be documented before they apply.
