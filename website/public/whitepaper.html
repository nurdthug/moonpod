Overview

MoonPod is a decentralized space-exploration project enabling global participants to collectively fund and govern lunar missions. Through community-owned Genesis Pods, participants secure space aboard lunar payloads, with blockchain providing ownership, access rights, mission records, and governance.

Phases

  1. Phase One: Own a Genesis Pod.
  2. Phase Two: Launch the $MOONPOD token with staking and governance.
  3. Phase Three: Deploy payloads with aerospace partners.
  4. Phase Four: Pursue moon-rock retrieval, sample distribution, and real-world returns.

$MOONPOD Token Strategy

The token launches aligned with mission milestones rather than rushed for immediate liquidity. Phase One has no token. Phase Two introduces $MOONPOD with governance and staking. The published whitepaper does not establish a final total supply, contract address, presale price, allocation table, caps, vesting, or liquidity plan — those are being finalized and will be disclosed when approved.

Technology

Ethereum mainnet smart contracts; ERC-721 Genesis Pods with evolving metadata; OpenZeppelin-based governance; possible future deployment to Polygon and Arbitrum; cryptographic proof of payload inclusion; mission tracking; certified aerospace logistics providers. These are target capabilities, not confirmation of deployed systems or partnerships.
